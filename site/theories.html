Theories of everything

Theories of everything

Every serious attempt to explain the whole universe, each told as a story anyone can read, and then set side by side. Whichever turns out right, a reader should be able to understand each one and see what changes between them.

The stories

  • TRPG: the universe as one full medium of relationships, told from the beginning.
  • The Standard Model, gravity and cosmology: The recipe that works: the best-tested description of nature, and the one every other theory must match.
  • Grand unification: One force in the beginning, which split apart as the universe cooled.
  • Asymptotic safety: Gravity needs nothing new, only a calm fixed point at the smallest scales.
  • String theory: Every particle is a tiny vibrating string, with gravity inside the mathematics.
  • Loop quantum gravity: Space is woven from threads, with a smallest possible area.
  • Octonions: The particles are written in the strangest number system, the one this framework is built on.
  • Causal sets: Space and time are separate events, and all that matters is which came before which.
  • The Wolfram Physics Project: A simple rule applied over and over to a web of connections.
  • Lisi's E8: Every particle in one beautiful shape, and what the mathematicians found when they checked.

Compare them

Each story is written the same way, so they can be read side by side. The comparison page sets out where the theories agree, where they differ, what each predicts and what would prove each one wrong.

2. Neutron-star lab

This solves the Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff equations and the tidal Love-number equation for quark matter, using the containment pressure B from the proton, the measured strong coupling, and a quark pairing gap Δ. Move either slider and the stars are solved again on your device. The results match the published script to the stated precision.

Mass against radius

Amber: your settings. Faint line: no pairing (Δ = 0) at the same B. Dashed line: the heaviest pulsar measured, PSR J0740+6620.

57.1 to 63.7 is the range allowed by the proton and Δ masses; 60.1 is the central value.

Solving…

Reference results from the script: with B = 60.1, Δ = 0 gives 1.72 M☉ and Λ₁.₄ = 224; Δ = 90 MeV gives 2.07 M☉ and Λ₁.₄ = 517. Both limits are met for Δ ≈ 82–97 MeV at the central B, and for Δ ≈ 75–108 MeV across the full range of B.

3. The complete script suite

The scripts below are the reference implementation. They require Python 3 with NumPy and SciPy; one also uses SymPy, and the workbook builders use pandas and openpyxl. To reproduce everything, download TRPG_All_Code.zip, unpack it, and run python run_all.py. Each script can also be run on its own with python -I scriptname.py.

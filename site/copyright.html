TRPG builds on established physics. The results, equations and measurements of the physicists and collaborations named on the Credits page belong to them and are cited, not claimed. Particle values come from the Particle Data Group, and physical constants from CODATA, under their own terms. Nothing on this site claims ownership of their discoveries.

Every part of physics helped

This framework required all of physics. Some of it is used unchanged, some is edited, and some is where the framework differs; every part helped the authors make the theory and bring it to completion, whether or not it is part of the final framework. The authors thank, in particular:

  • Classical physics: Galileo and Newton for motion and gravity; Lagrange and Hamilton for the principle of least action, the language of every equation on this site; Noether for the link between symmetry and conservation.
  • Electromagnetism and waves: Faraday and Maxwell, whose fields filling space are an ancestor of the full medium; d'Alembert, Fourier and Young for waves and interference.
  • Thermodynamics: Carnot, Clausius, Boltzmann and Gibbs; Bekenstein and Hawking for the entropy of black holes, which opened the road from entanglement to gravity.
  • Relativity: Einstein, Minkowski and Hilbert; Michelson and Morley, whose null result shaped how the framework's medium must behave.
  • Quantum physics: Planck, Einstein, Bohr, de Broglie, Heisenberg, Schrödinger, Born, Dirac and Pauli; Einstein, Podolsky, Rosen and Bell for entanglement; Aspect, Clauser and Zeilinger for testing it.
  • Quantum field theory and the Standard Model: Tomonaga, Schwinger, Feynman and Dyson; Yang and Mills; Glashow, Weinberg and Salam; Englert, Brout, Higgs, Guralnik, Hagen and Kibble; Gross, Wilczek and Politzer; and the experimental collaborations at CERN, Fermilab, SLAC, KEK and elsewhere.
  • The mathematics of division algebras and exceptional symmetry: Hamilton, Graves and Cayley; Hurwitz; Jordan, von Neumann and Wigner; Killing and Cartan; Freudenthal and Tits; Günaydin and Gürsey; Dixon, Furey, Dubois-Violette, Todorov, Krasnov and Boyle.
  • Grand unification and supersymmetry: Georgi, Glashow, Quinn, Weinberg, Pati and Salam; Wess and Zumino. Their running of the force strengths is used directly.
  • String theory: Veneziano, Nambu, Susskind, Green, Schwarz, Witten, Polchinski, Maldacena and the many who developed it. The framework does not use strings or extra dimensions, but string theory's brilliant perspective, its use of division algebras and exceptional groups, and the holographic link between entanglement and geometry, enabled the authors to better understand their own work.
  • Quantum gravity and emergent gravity: Ashtekar, Rovelli and Smolin (loop quantum gravity); Weinberg and Reuter (asymptotic safety); Sakharov, Jacobson, Verlinde, Van Raamsdonk, Ryu and Takayanagi.
  • Condensed matter: Kapitza, Landau, Tisza and London for superfluid helium, and Volovik for showing how much of particle physics appears in such a liquid.
  • Cosmology and astronomy: Friedmann, Lemaître, Hubble, Zwicky, Rubin, Penzias and Wilson, the Planck, DESI, SH0ES, LIGO, Virgo and NICER teams, and every observer whose measurements the framework is tested against.
  • Neutrinos and the far side: Pontecorvo, Minkowski, Yanagida, Gell-Mann, Ramond, Slansky, Mohapatra, Senjanović, Fukugita, Chikashige, Peccei, Nelson and Barr.
  • The Particle Data Group and CODATA, whose compiled values every calculation uses.
